Track your work. Own your data.

MyWorkTracking is a Chrome extension for logging daily work, todos, and follow-ups — with a Google Sheet in your own Drive as the only backend. No server, no account, no tracking.

What it does

Everything you need to log a working day

📋

Work, todos & follow-ups

Log entries with categories, priorities, flags and notes in seconds from the toolbar popup or the full app view.

📊

Dashboard & charts

See what you worked on, what's pending, and how your time breaks down — rendered with the Canvas API, no chart libraries.

🧾

PDF reports

Generate a clean, date-range report of your work history using Chrome's native Print to PDF — no third-party service involved.

📁

Google Sheets backend

One spreadsheet, a tab per month, created automatically in your own Google Drive. You can always open and edit it directly.

🔔

Toolbar reminders

A badge on the extension icon shows your pending todo and follow-up count for today, so nothing slips through.

🔒

No server, ever

Every request goes straight from your browser to Google's own APIs using your own sign-in. There's nothing for us to hold or lose.

How it works

Three steps, no setup on our end

Connect your Google Account

Sign in once. The extension requests access only to Google Sheets — never Gmail, Drive at large, or contacts.

Log your day

Add work items, todos and follow-ups as they happen. Each entry is written straight to your linked spreadsheet.

Review & report

Check your dashboard anytime, or generate a PDF report for any date range when you need to share your progress.

Data & privacy

✓

Your Sheet is the only database

MyWorkTracking has no backend of its own. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere except your own Google Sheet, and only you can see it unless you choose to share that spreadsheet yourself. No analytics, no ads, no selling data — ever.
