01

Overview

MyWorkTracking is a Chrome extension for logging daily work, todos, and follow-ups, using a Google Sheet you own as its only storage. This policy explains, in plain language, what the extension touches, where it goes, and what control you have over it.

The short answer is that there isn't much to explain: MyWorkTracking has no backend server. Every request it makes goes directly from your browser to Google's own APIs, using your own Google sign-in. We built it this way on purpose — it means your work data is never something we hold, and never something we could lose, leak, or sell even if we wanted to.

02

Information we collect

MyWorkTracking handles three narrow categories of information, all of which stay within Google's infrastructure under your own account:

Work data you enter

Descriptions, categories, priorities, flags, statuses, dates, and notes you type into the Add Work, Todo & Follow-up, or Work History screens are written directly to the Google Sheet you've linked. This data is stored in your Google Drive — the extension reads it back from there each time it shows you a dashboard, a report, or a list.

Extension settings

Your custom categories, custom flags, and the ID of your linked spreadsheet are saved using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync, which is tied to your Chrome profile and governed by Google's own account and sync policies — not by us.

Google authorization

When you click "Connect Google Account," Chrome's identity API requests a short-lived access token scoped only to the Google Sheets API (auth/spreadsheets). We never see your Google password, and we never request access to your Gmail, Drive files outside this app's scope, contacts, or any other Google product.

03

How it's used

Everything the extension collects is used for exactly one purpose: showing it back to you. Specifically, to:

  • Write the entries you submit to the correct monthly tab in your linked spreadsheet
  • Read those entries back to build your Dashboard, Work History table, and charts
  • Assemble the date-range report you generate under Reports, rendered entirely inside the extension
  • Remember your custom categories and flags so you don't re-create them each time

None of this involves a server we control. There is nowhere for this data to be "used" other than the extension rendering it back to you in your own browser.

04

What we don't do

  • ✓No server or database of our own — there is nothing for us to be breached, subpoenaed, or sold along with.
  • ✓No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • ✓No advertising, and no ad networks embedded anywhere in the extension.
  • ✓No selling, renting, or sharing your data with any third party — ever, under any circumstance.
  • ✓No access to any Google data beyond the Sheets scope you explicitly grant.
05

Third-party services

The only third party involved in MyWorkTracking is Google, through two of its APIs: the Google Sheets API (to store and read your work data) and Chrome's Identity API (to authenticate you). We don't integrate any other service — no analytics vendor, no crash reporter, no CDN, no third-party font or script host.

Because your data is ultimately stored in a Google Sheet, its handling there is also governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. We'd encourage reading it if you want the full picture of how Google, as your storage provider, treats that data.

06

Retention & deletion

Your data persists for exactly as long as it exists in your Google Sheet — which is to say, entirely under your control. Deleting a row, a monthly tab, or the spreadsheet itself removes that data immediately and completely; we hold no separate copy anywhere.

To stop the extension from accessing your account, open Settings and click Disconnect, or revoke access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Uninstalling the extension stops all activity but does not delete your spreadsheet — it's yours, and it stays yours.

07

Security

Access tokens are managed entirely by Chrome's identity API and are never written to disk by the extension itself. Every network request — to Google Sheets or to Google's authorization servers — travels over HTTPS. The extension's code runs under Chrome's Manifest V3 sandboxing, which blocks it from executing any remote or third-party script.

08

Children's privacy

MyWorkTracking is a work-management tool intended for general audiences managing their own professional tasks. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group.

09

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date at the top. Because the extension has no account system or email on file, we won't be able to notify you directly — checking back here before major updates is the reliable way to stay current.
